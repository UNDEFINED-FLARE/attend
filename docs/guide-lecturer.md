# Guide for lecturers

1. Sign up with the email your faculty administrator registered for you, and confirm it. Your modules appear
   under *My modules*.
2. **Start attendance.** Open a module, choose the venue, and press *Open session*. Choosing a mapped venue turns
   on the location check.
3. **Project the QR.** Use *Show full screen for projector*. The code changes every 20 seconds, so a photo sent to
   someone outside the hall stops working almost at once. The six-letter code under it is for students whose
   camera fails.
4. **Watch the register.** It updates live. *To review* means the evidence was weak (location off, very imprecise
   GPS, typed code). Those students are **not** absent.
5. **Correct records.** Use *Correct a record*. A reason is required and is kept in the audit log; the student
   is notified.
6. **Close the session.** Students who never checked in become *absent*. Pending reviews stay pending until you
   decide.
7. **Disputes.** Students can dispute an absent, late or pending record. Decide under *Disputes & reviews*.
8. **Reports.** The module page shows each student's attendance over resolved sessions, plus the *worst case* if
   every pending record were an absence. *Export CSV* downloads the register.
