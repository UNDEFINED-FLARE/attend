# Guide for students

1. **Sign up** with your university email, the one your faculty has on record, and confirm it. Your modules
   appear automatically. If they don't, open *Get set up* → *Check again*, or ask your faculty office.
2. **Check in:** point your phone camera at the QR code on the projector and open the link, or open UniAttend →
   *Check in* → *Scan QR code*. Allow location when asked. It takes a few seconds.
3. **If the camera won't work,** type the six-letter code under the QR. This may go to your lecturer for review.
4. **What the result means:**
   - *You're checked in*: recorded as present (or late).
   - *Sent for review*: your lecturer will confirm it. It does not count against you while pending.
   - *Not recorded*: the QR had expired. Scan the code currently on screen.
5. **History & disputes:** see every session, and dispute any absent, late or pending record with a short explanation.

**Privacy:** your location is used once at check-in and your coordinates are not stored, only how far you were
from the venue. Your phone is identified only by a one-way code. See the privacy notice for details.
