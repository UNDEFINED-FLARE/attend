You're offline

UniAttend couldn't reach the internet. Turn on mobile data or Wi-Fi, then try again.

Once you've opened UniAttend with signal, its main pages open without signal too, and check-ins made offline are saved on your phone and sent for your lecturer to confirm when you're back online.

Update needed

UniAttend needs a newer version of Android System WebView, the part of Android that shows app pages. It's free and takes a minute.

Update in the Play Store

After updating, close UniAttend and open it again. If the Play Store says there's no update, update Google Chrome instead, or ask your institution's IT support.
